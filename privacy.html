FosterLog Privacy Policy

Last updated 15 August 2026

The short version. FosterLog collects nothing. There is no account, no analytics and no server. Everything you record — children's names, notes, documents, photos, videos, mileage — stays on your own device and in your own iCloud account. We cannot see any of it, and neither can anyone else.

Who this is for

FosterLog is used by foster and kinship carers to keep records about children in their care. We understand what that means. The records you keep here are among the most sensitive a person can hold, and the app is built so that no copy of them ever reaches us.

What we collect

Nothing. There is no sign-up, no email address, no username, no device identifier and no usage tracking. We do not use analytics software, advertising software, crash reporting services or any third-party SDK that transmits data.

Where your records live

Everything you enter is stored on your iPhone or iPad. If you have iCloud turned on, your records also sync through your own private iCloud account so they appear on your other devices and survive a lost phone. That data belongs to you and is governed by Apple's privacy policy. We have no access to it — no server, no keys, no admin view.

Permissions the app asks for

  • Camera — only when you scan a document or record a short video for your records. Images and video are saved into FosterLog on your device and are never uploaded.
  • Microphone — only when you record a video with sound for a behaviour note. Audio stays with that recording on your device.
  • Location — only when you tag a mileage trip. The coordinates are stored with that trip entry on your device. Location is never collected in the background and never transmitted.
  • Face ID / Touch ID — only if you turn on the app lock. iOS returns a yes-or-no answer to FosterLog; the app never receives or stores biometric data.

Decline any of these and the rest of the app works normally.

Children's data

Records in FosterLog often concern children. Because the app transmits nothing, no child's information is ever collected by us, shared with anyone, or used for any purpose. FosterLog is a tool for the adult keeping the records; it is not directed at children and collects nothing from them.

What we share

Nothing, with anyone, ever. We hold no data, so there is nothing to sell, disclose, hand over or lose in a breach. You share a record only when you deliberately export or print it yourself.

Purchases

Any purchase is handled entirely by Apple. We never see your name, payment details or billing address — only whether this device holds a valid purchase.

Deleting your data

Delete a record in the app and it is gone from the device. Delete the app and its local data goes with it. To remove the synced copy, open Settings → [your name] → iCloud → Manage Account Storage, find FosterLog and delete its data there.

Your rights

Laws such as the GDPR and CCPA give you rights to access, correct, export and delete personal data held about you. We hold none, so there is nothing for us to produce or erase — your records are already entirely in your hands.

Changes

If this policy ever changes, the date at the top changes with it. Any change that affected what the app collects would be described plainly here first.
